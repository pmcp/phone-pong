How to play

  • Lay the phones top to top. Drag anywhere to move your paddle.
  • Hit the ball off the top of your screen and it lands on the other phone.
  • Swipe while you hit to put spin on it.
  • Bonuses fall from the top. Hit them with the ball, not the paddle. Below the dashed line they break.
  • Bonuses go into your two slots. Tap a slot to use it. The same bonus twice stacks: stronger or longer.
  • 2 to 5 phones. Everyone has lives (5 each with two players, 3 with more). Miss and you lose one. Last one standing wins.
  • With more than two players, tap a red bonus and pick who gets it.
